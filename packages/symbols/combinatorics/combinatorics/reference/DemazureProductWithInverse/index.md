- Takes a `Permutation` and returns a `Permutation` — a typed map, so a wrong carrier is a type error rather than a wrong answer.
- FindStat's Mp00159: π⁻¹ ⋆ π, the inverse on the left, folded over a reduced word of π. Not injective: it is a surjection onto the involutions.
- Also takes a `SignedPermutation`, returning a `SignedPermutation`: The Demazure product π⁻¹ ⋆ π in the hyperoctahedral group.
- FindStat's Mp00260: π⁻¹ ⋆ π, the inverse on the left. Not injective: it is a surjection onto the involutions. The group is generated by the adjacent transpositions and the sign change of the largest value.
