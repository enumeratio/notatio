---
name: ResolutionQuiverMonoidSize
domain: Dyck path statistics
signature: ResolutionQuiverMonoidSize(path)
summary: "The number of elements of the monoid generated by the map of the resolution quiver of the path's linear Nakayama algebra: the depth plus the least common multiple of its cycle lengths."
---

- Defined over `DyckPath` as an expression in `_x`, evaluated by compute-engine — the definition IS the implementation.
- Takes a `DyckPath` and nothing else — it reads values against their positions, or walks the orbits, so it needs the bijection. Applying it to a bare list is a type error, not a wrong answer.
- FindStat's St001505: the powers of the map are distinct until the depth and then repeat with the period.
